Insurance we work with

Insurance and payment

Which insurance are you in-network with?

Anthem BCBS and United Healthcare/UMR. Tricare is out-of-network. Please check with your insurer that we are in-network on your specific plan.

Can I be a patient without insurance?

Of course. We have many self-pay patients. Initial visits are $250 and follow-ups $150, due in full on the day.

Your first appointment

What do I need to complete?

Intake paperwork at least 24 hours prior, with your driver's license and insurance card uploaded. Otherwise the appointment will be rescheduled.

In-person or telehealth?

We offer both.

How often will I see my provider?

New patients return after 2 weeks, then monthly until well controlled, then most patients every 3 months.

ADHD and medications

Do you perform ADHD testing?

Yes. FDA-approved, computerized ADHD testing for ages 7 and up, $250, not billed to insurance.

Will I be prescribed a stimulant without prior records?

Not unless you have a prior diagnosis letter, neuropsychological testing documentation, or an evaluation with our provider plus completed computerized testing.

How often are controlled medications prescribed?

You need an appointment at least every 90 days.

Will you order bloodwork?

Comprehensive labs are encouraged and drawn at any LabCorp location, billed to your insurance by LabCorp. Bring results from the past year to your first visit.
