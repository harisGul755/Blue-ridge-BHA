Welcome to Blue Ridge Behavioral Health Associates

We are pleased to offer psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis, and medication management for most major mental health concerns. We treat patients age 10 years and up. Common diagnoses include ADHD, emotional and mood disorders, anxiety disorders, depression, and adjustment disorders.

Whether this is your first time seeking mental health care, or you are looking for a second opinion on an established diagnosis, we welcome you with open arms.

Our approach

Holistic

We view each patient as the whole individual, rather than a single symptom or diagnosis to be treated.

Thorough

We examine biological, psychological, environmental and social components, paired with laboratory and psychological testing.

Collaborative

We partner with you or your family on a treatment plan that fits your goals.

What we treat

ADHD

Computerized, FDA-approved testing for ages 7 and up.

Anxiety and depression

Medication management with lifestyle guidance and therapy referral.

Mood and adjustment

Support through stress, crises and life transitions.
