Linda Sher, PMHNP-C, FNP-BC

Psychiatric Mental Health & Family Nurse Practitioner

As one of the first board-certified nurse practitioners in Loudoun County, Linda has a particular passion for bringing personalized patient care to her community. After nearly 30 years of managing patients in local hospitals, she brings her love of psychiatry to the outpatient setting.

Linda is committed to staying at the forefront of medical advancements, valuing both traditional and innovative approaches, and is an active member of several professional organizations. Outside work she enjoys time with family, long hikes, gardening and travel.

Catriona Timpe, PMHNP-BC, FNP-C

Psychiatric Mental Health & Family Nurse Practitioner

Catriona brings over 19 years of nursing experience in both inpatient and outpatient settings. As a Nurse Practitioner in acute care she developed a strong clinical foundation in high-pressure environments, then moved into psychiatric mental health, blending medical expertise with a passion for emotional wellness.

She specializes in anxiety, depression, ADHD, mood disorders, trauma, and interpersonal challenges, using a holistic, evidence-based approach and collaborating with patients, therapists and other healthcare professionals. At the core of her practice are empathy, respect and empowerment.

Cinthia Lyvers, PMHNP-BC, FNP-C

Psychiatric Mental Health & Family Nurse Practitioner

Cinthia approaches mental health care through both a medical and human lens. Her background in family practice and behavioral health taught her that true wellness begins with understanding the whole person: their story, lifestyle, and goals.

She cares for children, teens, and adults with anxiety, depression, or difficulty coping with life's transitions, combining evidence-based medication management with supportive education and practical strategies. She honors both the science of psychiatry and the art of compassionate care.
